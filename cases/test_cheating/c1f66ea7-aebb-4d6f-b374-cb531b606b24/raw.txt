The accused (Arjun) approached the victim (Meera) offering to sell a car. He claimed that the car was in good condition and showed photos. Based on this, Meera transferred ₹5 lakhs to Arjun via bank transfer. Arjun promised delivery within 7 days. After receiving the money, Arjun stopped responding. The car was never delivered. Later investigation revealed that Arjun did not own the car at the time of the transaction. Arjun claims that he intended to deliver the car but the deal failed due to issues with the actual owner.
