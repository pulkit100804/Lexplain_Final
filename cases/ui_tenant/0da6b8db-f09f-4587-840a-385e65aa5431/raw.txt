On the night of 12th August, the accused and the victim were involved in a heated argument outside a local shop. According to a witness present at the scene, the argument escalated quickly, and in a moment of anger, the accused picked up a wooden stick lying nearby and struck the victim on the head.  The victim immediately collapsed and was taken to the hospital by nearby individuals. Medical reports later confirmed that the victim had suffered a severe head injury and died the following morning.  There was no prior enmity reported between the accused and the victim. The incident appeared to have occurred spontaneously during the altercation.  The police recovered the wooden stick from the scene, and blood stains matching the victim were found on it. The witness stated that the accused did not appear to plan the attack but acted suddenly during the argument.  The accused was arrested the next day.
